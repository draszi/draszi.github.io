Privacy Policy

Updated November 13, 2026

Your calendar and reminders

With your permission, Brief uses iOS to read calendar events and reminder lists so it can display your agenda and Live Activity. When you create, edit, delete, or complete an item in Brief, the change is saved to the corresponding calendar or reminder list through iOS. Your calendar and reminder providers may sync those items according to the accounts and sync settings you use on your device.

What Brief stores

Brief saves preferences, calendar and reminder snapshots, memos, memo archives, and memo recovery copies locally. The app and its Live Activity extension share local data so the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island can show selected content. Anyone who can see those surfaces may be able to read the information displayed there. You can choose which calendars and reminder lists appear, turn off the memo tab for the Live Activity, or stop the Live Activity in Brief.

Brief does not send your agenda or memos to the developer or an analytics service. Brief has no account sign-in, advertising, or tracking. Brief uses the system clipboard when you choose Copy or Paste.

Your choices and deletion

You can revoke Calendar or Reminders access in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security. Brief stops using the corresponding local snapshots when it detects that access has changed. Brief refreshes snapshots as your sources change. Memo drafts and archives stay on your device until you clear or delete them. A local recovery file may retain the last memo you cleared until it is replaced or Brief's app data is removed.

Deleting Brief removes its local app data from the device. Calendar events and reminders belong to their source accounts and can be managed in Calendar or Reminders. Copies in device backups are managed through your backup settings.

Apple services

The tutorial video plays from the app. Download links open Apple's iCloud and Shortcuts services. Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. Brief verifies purchase status on your device and does not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide sales reports and platform diagnostics according to its own policies. TestFlight users may also choose to send feedback through Apple.
